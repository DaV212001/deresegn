Statutory Balancing Formula
1. Net Subtotal: Gross Turnover − Line Item Discounts
2. Taxable Base: Sum of VAT 15% Items + Excise Tax
3. Output VAT (15%): Taxable Base × 0.15 (Truncated to 2 d.p.)
4. Grand Total: Taxable Base + Exempt + Zero-Rated + Output VAT
5. Net Payable: Grand Total − Statutory Withheld Tax (3%)

Withholding Tax Architecture: Buyer vs Seller Roles
05 / 10
STATUTORY LEGAL PRINCIPLE (Income Tax Proclamation No. 979/2016, Article 92):
The BUYER (Designated Withholding Agent) issues the Withholding Tax Receipt, NOT the seller. The seller cannot withhold taxes on themselves.
Automated Threshold Intelligence
  • Statutory Thresholds: ≥ 20,000 ETB for goods; ≥ 10,000 ETB for services.
  • Adaptive Smart UI: Below threshold or B2C sales, withholding controls completely disappear to eliminate clutter.
  • Auto-Calculation: Surfaces 3% card at statutory threshold, computes pre-tax deduction, and adjusts Net Payable.
5-Step Lifecycle Workflow
1. Invoice Issuance: Seller issues invoice quoting 3% withholding.
2. Net Settlement: Buyer pays Net Commercial Payable (97%).
3. Tax Remittance: Buyer pays 3% to MoR and files WHT receipt.
4. Cloud Link: MoR links WHT to Seller's original Invoice IRN.
5. Certificate Retrieval: Seller accesses buyer's WHT certificate in-app.

MoR Rule 7020 Mandate

Under MoR Rule 7020, Credit Notes (CRE) and Debit Notes (DEB) cannot invent arbitrary items. Every returned line item must correspond exactly to the parent sales invoice:

  • Line descriptions and unit prices must match parent.
  • Adjustment quantity bounded by original sales volume.
  • Tax categories (VAT15, Exempt, Zero) must remain consistent.
Automated Deresegn Enforcement
  • One-Tap Action: "Issue Credit Note" directly from parent invoice details.
  • Locked Reference Card: Auto-populates and locks Parent IRN, syncing buyer details.
  • Line Item Cloning: Automatically copies parent items into adjustment table.
  • Fail-Safe Math: Negative adjustments automatically rebalance total VAT liability.
